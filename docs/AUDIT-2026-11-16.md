## Critical findings

### C1. Homepage hero shows "$NaN" as the freight price
Live text: "every order ships by hydraulic tail-lift truck to your club, estate or farm for a flat $NaN."
Cause: `src/components/HeroSlider.jsx:40` uses `money(F.ship)`, but `F` (line 26) has no `ship` key.
This sits in the first screen and in the first 150 words Google and AI crawlers read.

### C2. Vehicle-only claims are hardcoded on all 157 product pages
`app/shop/[category]/[slug]/ProductDetailClient.jsx` prints these on every product:
- "5-Yr LiFePO4 Warranty" and "State Road Legal Kit, QLD / NSW / VIC pre-filled" (lines 95 to 108).
- Fallbacks "High Output AC Electric" and "Full 36+ Holes Range" when a product has no motor or range (lines 40 to 42).

Checked live on 8 non-vehicle products: all 8 show all four. Examples: a golf driver, a Bushnell rangefinder, a lead-acid battery and a spare wheel each claim a lithium warranty, a road-legal kit and an AC motor.
This breaks two project rules: no spec on golf gear unless the product record carries it, and the 5-year lithium warranty applying to lithium packs only.

### C3. 106 products share one boilerplate description
- 106 of 157 products use the same long description: "The [name] provides high quality performance for Australian golfers and property owners. Built with durable materials and supported by local Australian technical service."
- 113 of 157 use the same short description: "[name] available at The Buggy Shop Australia with Australian warranty and fast nationwide shipping."
- Median product description is 28 words. The same text feeds the Product schema `description`.
- The boilerplate also promises "technical service", which conflicts with the no-servicing rule (see W10).

Affected: accessories 23, luxury carts 21, off-road 20, batteries 20, parts 17, golf clubs 7, kids 3, push 2.
The crosscheck's duplicate test misses it because the product name makes each hash unique.

### C4. robots.txt lets AI crawlers into the private paths
The 12 named AI bots each get their own group containing only `Allow: /`. Under the robots standard a bot obeys its most specific group only, so GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and the rest are **not** bound by the `Disallow` lines for /admin/, /order/, /checkout/ and the thank-you pages. Those pages do carry noindex, so the risk is crawl waste and exposure, not indexing.

Also in the same file:
- `Content-Signal: ... ai-train=no` contradicts `Allow: /` for training crawlers (GPTBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, Bytespider). Pick one stance.
- The list is dated: `Claude-Web` is retired; `OAI-SearchBot`, `Claude-User`, `Claude-SearchBot`, `Perplexity-User`, `Meta-WebIndexer` are missing.
- Commit ae295d7 says the AI blocks were removed, but `scripts/gen-agent-files.mjs:82` still generates them.

Simplest fix: drop the per-bot groups (the `*` group already allows them) or repeat the disallows in each.

### C5. Open Graph tags are wrong or inconsistent
- `og:url` points to the homepage on 6 pages: /faq/, /wholesale/, /shipping/, /returns/, /privacy/, /terms/ (inherited from `app/layout.tsx`).
- `og:description` and `twitter:description` differ from the meta description on 137 of 294 pages. The homepage OG text still says "luxury golf buggies for sale", which is /shop/'s keyword.
- `og:type` is missing on home, category, brand and location pages.
- `og:updated_time` is emitted as `<meta name=...>` not `property=`, so no parser reads it, and its value is the build time rather than the real change date.

## Lastmod

- 293 of 294 sitemap URLs carry `2026-10-06`; the homepage carries `2026-10-07`. With one date everywhere, lastmod tells Google nothing.
- `scripts/gen-lastmod.mjs` only moves a date when run locally and the JSON is committed. Vercel builds skip it by design.
- Hashes cover page data, not templates. A change to the product template (such as fixing C2) will not move any product's lastmod.
- All 25 blog posts show `dateModified` 2026-10-06 and share just four publish dates.
- `changefreq` and `priority` are ignored by Google; "daily" on / and /shop/ is not true. Safe to remove.
- No `og:updated_time` or `article:modified_time` property exists on any page.

## Warnings

**W1. Thin pages.** 30 brand pages have 146 to 250 words. /wholesale/ has 82 words and is in the sitemap although `PAGES.wholesale` is false. 11 subcategories are under 250 words.

**W2. Internal linking.** No orphans. 39 pages have exactly one inbound link, mostly brand pages reached only from /brands/. Product pages do not link to their own brand page (checked: the Cougar cart links to Clicgear, Club Car and MGI, not Cougar).

**W3. Structured data.**
- Plaintext email in JSON-LD on 6 pages (/, /about/, /contact/, /shipping/, /returns/, /privacy/). Project rule says entity-encode.
- BlogPosting `publisher.logo` points to /images/logo.png, which returns 404.
- No `sameAs` anywhere; `BRAND.sameAs` is empty.
- Product schema has no `shippingDetails` or `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`. Google flags both in Merchant listings.
- 9 products have brand "Various".
- Store schema carries AggregateRating 4.9 from 6,340 reviews. Google treats a business's own rating markup as self-serving and shows no stars for it.
- 35 of 59 FAQ questions repeat across pages; one appears on 8.
- City pages have breadcrumb schema only.

**W4. Meta descriptions.** 28 end in "…" (cut by `clampAtWord` in `lib/seo.js`). 45 fall outside the 130 to 155 rule: 27 are 156 to 160, 18 are 110 to 129. Three golf-club product titles are generic ("Golf Driver | The Buggy Shop").

**W5. /search/ is indexable.** Both /search/ and /search/?q= return index, follow. Internal search results should be noindex.

**W6. Redirect chains.** `http://www` takes two hops to reach the apex. So does `www` without a trailing slash. Minor.

**W7. Page weight.** HTML is 495 to 660 KB raw (48 to 78 KB compressed). /shop/ and every city page ship about 1.05 MB of raw JavaScript and a 344 to 354 KB data payload, because city pages embed the full catalogue.

**W8. Bing and IndexNow.** `bingVerification` is still "pending", so no Bing meta tag exists. The IndexNow key file is live, but nothing in the repo submits URLs, so IndexNow is not in use.

**W9. Agent files.**
- Five extensionless `.well-known` files are served as `application/octet-stream`. This is a known Vercel limit, not a config bug.
- llms.txt states a "3-Year Chassis & Powertrain Warranty" for the whole site; only 2 product records carry it.
- llms.txt omits Pay in 4, which the site offers. CLAUDE.md lists a different set again.
- /favicon.ico returns 404; no web manifest.

**W10. Service and workshop wording.** The project rule says no repair or servicing is offered. The site says otherwise in several places: "Delivery & Technical Service" on all 8 city pages, "Queensland Workshop & Dispatch Hub" in the header, "certified technicians" and "service heritage" in `BRAND.differentiation`, and 106 product descriptions. This needs your decision on what is true.

**W11. Latent items.**
- `src/config/core.js:127` has the chat email on the wrong domain (thebuggyshop.com.au). It is not rendered on / or /contact/ today.
- Cart and checkout contain a "FREE Freight" branch that would show if the fee were ever 0.
- 13 files show as modified in git from line endings only. A `.gitattributes` would stop that.
